Coastline has a large support system, and almost none of it costs you anything. Using it early is what successful online students do differently.

Accommodations and disability services

Your experience in this class is important to me, and it is the policy and practice of Coastline College to create inclusive and accessible learning environments consistent with federal and state law.

Special Programs and Services coordinates academic adjustments and auxiliary aids for students with disabilities. Accommodations are established through an interactive process among you, me, and a Special Programs counselor.

iNote

1515 Monrovia Ave., Newport Beach, CA 92663 · (714) 241-6214 · specialprograms@coastline.edu Office hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. · coastline.edu/specialprograms (opens in a new tab) (opens in a new tab)

If you experience barriers in this course, or have a temporary or permanent disability that requires accommodation and have not yet established services, contact them for a confidential intake interview. Accommodations are not retroactive, so please do not wait; reach out in week one if you can.

Financial aid

All students, including those awaiting financial aid, must have course materials before class begins, which for this course means there is nothing to buy. Tuition balances are paid before remaining funds are disbursed. Military and Contract Ed students who cannot wait for disbursement should contact Financial Aid.

Withdrawals

You assume responsibility for completing every course you register for, and for officially dropping any course you stop attending. Check your Student Class Program (Web Schedule Bill) in MyCCC (opens in a new tab) (opens in a new tab), under the Student tab in Other Student Resources, for this course's refund, drop, and "W" deadlines.

Once the final withdrawal deadline passes, you receive a grade whether or not you completed the course, which can mean an F on your permanent record. If you are considering dropping, message me first; sometimes there is a version of the term that still works.
