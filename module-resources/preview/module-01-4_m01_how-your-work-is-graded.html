How Your Work Is Graded

On this page

  • Two shared rubrics
  • What the Assignment Rubric measures
  • The midterm and project
  • Grade category weights
  • How to use the rubrics and find feedback

Every scored quiz, assignment, lab, case-study forum, midterm part, and project submission is worth 100 points. Introductions is complete/incomplete. The learning contract is worth 1 point; feedback surveys and appendix practice remain ungraded.

Two shared rubrics

  • Assignment Rubric: all labs, incident reports and repairs, written analyses, both midterm parts, and project submissions.
  • Forum Rubric: the seven case-study discussions, each with an initial post and peer reply.

Each rubric has five criteria worth 20 points each, scored at None (0), Partial 1 (10), Partial 2 (15), or Full (20). The same rubric is reused in Canvas for every activity of its type.

What the Assignment Rubric measures

  1. Completeness and scope: complete the deliverables that the activity requests.
  2. Correctness and verification: show the solution, analysis, or design meets its requirements and provide the requested checks.
  3. Concepts and controls: name the required principles, weakness classes, and safeguards precisely.
  4. Reasoning and evidence: support conclusions and decisions with specific evidence, sources, and reasoning.
  5. Communication and accountability: write clearly and include required disclosure, reflection, citations, and attribution.

The assignment page defines the specific evidence needed. Incident reports require a repair with hostile and legitimate tests. Labs require setup or investigation evidence. Written analyses require supported conclusions. Project drafts and team finals require coherent designs and verification plans; individual builds require personal test results and a decision under challenge. Read the project expectations for its required safeguards and evidence.

The midterm and project

The Module 08 midterm has two submissions: a timed practical and a written breach analysis, each worth 100 points. Canvas states the practical's time limit and window; its instructions state the assessment conditions.

The project has three submissions worth 100 points each: the Module 12 draft, the Module 16 team final, and the Module 16 individual build with its decision under challenge. They count equally within Assignments. Team attribution and each student's own evidence distinguish shared work from individual work.

Grade category weights

Quizzes contribute 40%, Assignments 40%, and Forums 20% of the final grade. The midterm and project belong to Assignments and have no separate category weight. Points earned within a category determine that category's contribution.

How to use the rubrics and find feedback

Read the attached rubric and activity instructions before starting. Compare your submission with the Full descriptions and check every required deliverable before submitting. Canvas feedback may appear in rubric scores, file annotations, or assignment comments. Details are on the Communication, Interactions & Course Guidelines page.
