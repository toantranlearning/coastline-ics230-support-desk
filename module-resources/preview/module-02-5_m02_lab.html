What you are doing, and why

You open your own copy of the TechCorp customer portal in Google Cloud Shell, sign in, change two lines, watch the portal update, undo the change, and write a short Markdown report with three captures. The lab practices the edit loop (change a file, save, reload) and the reset, both from the tutorial panel, and the report format from the Writing in Markdown page. Plan about an hour.

Use the Lab Guide and Cloud Shell Guide to set up or reopen your environment. This assignment specifies what to change and capture.

Before you launch

  • Sign in to your Google account in this browser first. The launch link only works once you are signed in. If you click it signed out, sign in on the page you land on and click the link again.
  • You do not need a credit card or a paid trial. If a banner offers "$300 in free credits" or asks for a card, close it.
  • Click the launch link once. Clicking it again later makes a second copy of the project. The start command removes extra copies, so nothing is lost, but next time reopen your Cloud Shell tab instead.

Launch the environment

Click Launch Course Environment (opens in a new tab). If clicking does nothing, paste this address into your browser; the address is the button:

https://shell.cloud.google.com/cloudshell/open?cloudshell_git_repo=https://github.com/toantranlearning/coastline-ics230-support-desk&cloudshell_tutorial=TUTORIAL.md

The first time, Cloud Shell shows a welcome panel (click Continue, and Authorize if asked) and then asks "Do you trust this repository?" (click Confirm). It copies the portal in and opens a tutorial panel on the right.

The tutorial helps insert the start command; press Enter in the shell to run it. Then work through its three sections, See the portal, Change something and see it update, and Undo a change and reset. The steps below follow those three sections and tell you what to capture at each one.

Coming back to your environment

You launch it once from this page. After that, you reopen your Cloud Shell tab and run bash scripts/start.sh. Reopening, resetting, the limit on hours, and fixing problems are all on one page: Working in Google Cloud Shell, in the Course Orientation module. Bookmark that page.

Your environment is your copy and the only system you are authorized to test.

The lab

  1. See the portal. Run the start command on the tutorial's first page, then open the portal with Web Preview on port 8080 and sign in with the support-rep account.
    • Username rmarsh, password password. These are development credentials; never reuse them anywhere.
    • You land on the customer list, with TechCorp Portal in the bar at the top.
    • Copy the line the start command printed, the one that says the portal is running on port 8080. It goes in your report.
    • Capture 1: the signed-in customer list, showing TechCorp Portal. This confirms the environment works before you change anything.
  2. Change something and see it update. Make two changes, one in each file, as the tutorial shows for the first.
    • In layout.php, line 34 reads <span class="brand">TechCorp Portal</span>. Change TechCorp Portal to a label of your own, for example Rae's Support Desk.
    • In index.php, line 23 reads <h2>Customers</h2>. Change it to <h2>My Customers</h2>.
    • Save each file (Ctrl-S, or Cmd-S on a Mac) and reload the portal tab. The top bar shows your label and the heading reads My Customers. You restarted nothing; the server reads your files on every page load.
    • Capture 2: the portal showing both changes.
  3. Undo a change and reset. Put both files back the way the tutorial shows.
    • Open the Source Control panel. It lists the two files you changed; clicking one shows the old line and the new line side by side.
    • Hover the Changes heading and click Discard All Changes. Reload the portal tab. The bar reads TechCorp Portal and the heading reads Customers again.
    • The other reset options, from gentle to total, are on the Working in Google Cloud Shell page.
    • Capture 3: the portal back to its original name and heading.
  4. Write the report. Create reports/m01-setup.md in your environment and write it as the Writing in Markdown page shows.
    • Start with the NAME / AI DISCLOSURE / REFLECTION block from "What to submit" below.
    • Then one ## heading per step above, in order. Under each, one or two sentences on what you did and what you saw, and the image reference for that step's capture.
    • Under step 1, put the line the start command printed in a fenced text block.
    • Preview the file as you write. Then download the report and the three image files; the page shows where Download is.
    • Keep the report and evidence together and submit the required files. Your course submission is the preservation record for this work.

If your environment will not launch or you cannot sign in, write the report anyway: what you tried, what you saw, and what you plan to do next. Post the same in the Q&A discussion or message me so it gets sorted out.

Open notes and open book are both fine. Respond in your own words; do not copy from other sources.

AI use on this assignment. The syllabus policy governs; these are its terms for a setup lab.

✓Permitted, with disclosure

  • Asking an AI to explain a concept, an OWASP guideline, or a PHP error message in general terms.
  • A grammar or spelling checker on your own writing.
  • Generating or refactoring code, proposing tests, and planning or editing the report, with disclosure of the tool, help requested, material used or changed, and checks performed. Report only results you observed.

✕Not permitted

  • Submitting AI-generated code, analysis, or writing without disclosing it.
  • Pointing an AI-assisted tool at any target other than your own course environment.

You must be able to explain every line you submit, on request. Undisclosed AI use is handled as academic dishonesty under the syllabus.

Expected output

Your captures will differ in cosmetic detail; the behavior must not. The lab passes when all of the following are true in your own environment:

  • The launch link opened Cloud Shell with the portal copied in, and the start command reported the portal running on port 8080.
  • The rmarsh account signed in and reached the customer list (capture 1).
  • Both files changed, and the portal showed your label and My Customers (capture 2).
  • Discard All Changes returned the portal to TechCorp Portal and Customers (capture 3).
  • The report has a heading for each step, the start command's line in a fenced block, and the three images referenced.

What to submit

Submit reports/m01-setup.md as a Markdown (.md) file, written as the Writing in Markdown page in the Course Orientation module shows. Put the start command's line in a fenced block. Upload the three image files with the report.

At the top of the report, include this block, completed:

NAME: First Last
AI DISCLOSURE: What you asked an AI and what you used, or "None."
REFLECTION: Two or three sentences: what you set up, what was
difficult, and what you learned.
