Class Announcements

This forum is where course-wide news lands: schedule adjustments, clarifications that affect everyone, lab or tooling issues and their fixes, and department items worth your attention such as scholarships, events, and career fairs. It is the one place you can check to know whether anything has changed.

Read it because it is useful.

What you should do

Check it with your Inbox and your Coastline email, every day. The Communication, Interactions & Course Guidelines page asks for daily checks for a reason: deadlines shift, the course environment changes, a lab step needs a correction. An announcement you read three days late is an announcement that did not help you.

Set your notifications so this reaches you. In Canvas, go to Account → Notifications and set Announcement to notify you immediately. Do that in week one, before there is anything urgent to miss.

You may reply here

Replies are open, and the class sees them. That is deliberate: if you are confused by an announcement, several other people are too, and the answer helps all of them at once.

Reply here when your question is about the announcement itself and the answer would be useful to everyone. A clarification about a moved deadline, a follow-up on a tooling fix, or a "does this apply to the Module 07 scan reading as well?" all belong here.

What does not belong in this forum. Anything about your grade, your submission, or your circumstances goes to the Canvas Inbox instead. So does anything about a specific quiz item, since posting it publicly would advantage students who have not taken the quiz yet. The routing table on the Communication, Interactions & Course Guidelines page covers every case; when in doubt, use the Inbox and I will move the question here myself if the class should see it.

What to expect from me

I post here when something changes or when something is worth your time. I do not post filler, so an announcement from me is worth opening. I answer replies on the same schedule as the Inbox, described on the communication page.

If a change affects a deadline, it will appear here and as a change to the Canvas due date. The due date in Canvas is always authoritative. If this forum and the calendar ever disagree, follow the calendar and tell me about the discrepancy.
