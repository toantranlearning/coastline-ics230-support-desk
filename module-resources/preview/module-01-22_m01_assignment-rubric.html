Assignment Rubric

This shared rubric grades all labs, incident reports and repairs, written analyses, both midterm parts, and project submissions. Five criteria worth 20 points each total 100 points. The assignment page defines the required scope, evidence, and assessment conditions. Grade only what that activity requires; written analyses do not require a code repair, and design drafts require a verification plan rather than a completed build.

Table. Columns: Criterion, None (0), Partial 1 (10), Partial 2 (15), Full (20).
CriterionNone (0)Partial 1 (10)Partial 2 (15)Full (20)
1. Completeness and scopeNo relevant submission.Some required tasks completed; major gaps.Most required tasks completed; minor gaps.All required deliverables are complete, accurate, and within the assigned scope.
2. Correctness and verificationWork is incorrect or unsupported.Major errors or checks missing.Mostly correct with gaps in verification.The solution, analysis, or design meets the stated requirements. Required checks support it; repairs stop hostile behavior while preserving legitimate use, builds work, and plans specify testable expected results.
3. Concepts and controlsConcepts absent or misapplied.Relevant ideas named vaguely.Correct concepts and controls with minor gaps.Required principles, weakness classes, and controls are named precisely and connected to the task; include CWE and OWASP references when requested.
4. Reasoning and evidenceNo justification or evidence.Generic claims or weak support.Reasoning mostly supported, with gaps.Conclusions and decisions are justified against specific observations or cited sources. Evidence is labeled, limitations are acknowledged, and required objections or tradeoffs are addressed.
5. Communication and accountabilityMissing or unreadable.Disorganized or attribution missing.Mostly clear with minor omissions.Organized and readable, with required identity, disclosure, reflection, citations, and attribution. The submission follows the activity's collaboration and assistance rules.

Score: ___ / 100

For incident reports, use the required TC-IR-01 sections and before-and-after proof. For setup labs, show the required environment checks. For breach and scan analyses, support conclusions with sources or supplied evidence. For team designs, show coherent decisions and a verification plan; for individual builds, show personal test evidence and the required decision under challenge.
