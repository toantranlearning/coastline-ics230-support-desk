How due dates work

  • Two modules run per week. Each module runs Monday 12:00 a.m. through Sunday 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time.
  • On a module that has a forum, the forum post is due Friday 11:59 p.m. and your reply to a classmate is due Sunday 11:59 p.m. The forum needs two turns, which is why the first half is due earlier. Forums run in Module 01 (the introductions) and on the odd-numbered Modules 03 to 15.
  • Everything else (quiz and assignment) is due Sunday 11:59 p.m.
  • Quizzes and the midterm practical close on the due date. No late window.
  • Assignments are accepted up to 7 days late at 10% per day. The full late-work policy is on the Syllabus page.

Canvas is the authority on dates. Check the Assignments page or your Canvas Calendar for the exact timestamp on any item, and set your notification preferences so reminders reach you.

The 16-module schedule

Eleven modules deliver a two-part assignment: an incident report on the TechCorp TC-IR-01 form, plus the hands-on fix it documents, both submitted together as one Markdown file. The other modules say what they deliver instead.

From Module 09 to Module 15, each assignment page also states your team's design project increment for that module. It goes in the team's design document or your build report, not in the incident report.

An appendix on race conditions and concurrency follows Module 16: a lecture, an optional forum, and a practice quiz. None of it is graded.

Two deadlines to put in your calendar now

  • Module 08: the midterm. The timed practical, taken in Canvas without assistance (one seeded flaw in your own portal copy from the Modules 01 to 07 material: diagnose it, repair it, prove it, and report it), plus the take-home written breach analysis. Module 08 also has its own incident report and the design project kickoff. Plan the time for it in advance.
  • Module 12: the design project draft. Your team's design document is assembled and submitted for feedback here, with each member's build so far, and the final is due at Module 16.
