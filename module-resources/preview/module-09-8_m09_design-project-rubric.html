Grades the collaborative secure-design project: the partner REST API the team designs together and each member builds as running code. The design lives in one team document in three sections (authentication; authorization and data; operations), grown from Module 08, with a draft due at Module 12 and the final at Module 16. Each member also builds the API under api/ in their own portal copy and submits a build report with a decision-under-challenge section, which is graded per student.

Weight in the course

The design project is one grading category worth 20 points of the course grade, split three ways:

  • Draft (Module 12): 5 points. The assembly checkpoint, graded lightly on a real assembled document with honest attribution and the build evidence so far.
  • Team design document (Module 16): 5 points. Graded on criteria 1 through 4 below.
  • Individual build with the decision under challenge (Module 16): 10 points. Each member's running API, its curl evidence, and their decision-under-challenge section, graded on criterion 5 below and the per-category evidence under criterion 2.

Criteria 1 through 4 grade the team document. Criterion 5 grades each student. The build's evidence is where criterion 2 is verified per member: a safeguard is credited only when the curl request and response show it working.

The criteria

Five criteria at 20 points each, 100 total. Levels: None 0 · Partial 1 = 10 · Partial 2 = 15 · Full = 20.

Evidence table for criterion 2

A safeguard earns credit under criterion 2 only when the evidence shows it working. Each category the project builds gets one row: the safeguard, and the curl result that proves it. The required core is graded for every project; the electives are graded only where the design chose to build them.

Evidence table for criterion 2. Columns: Category, Safeguard, Evidence that proves it.
CategorySafeguardEvidence that proves it
Authentication (required)API-key authenticationA request with no key returns 401; a valid key in the header returns 200.

What the attribution table is for

The attribution table, which member authored and built which part, is what makes the individual grade defensible. A member who authored nothing and built nothing has nothing of their own to be graded on under criterion 5.

Where the scores are recorded

The project category retains its 20% course weight. Its assignment points are draft 5, team final 5, and individual build/decision 10, so these items contribute in the stated ratio. Criteria 1–4 guide the team-design evaluation; criterion 5 and each member's actual evidence guide the individual evaluation. The native Canvas assignments separate the team final from the individual build.
