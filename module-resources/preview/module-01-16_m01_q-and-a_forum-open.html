Q&A Discussion

Ask here when the question is about the course and the answer would help other students: a step that does not work as the page says, an error message you cannot read, a deadline you are unsure about. Other students almost certainly have the same question, and answering each other is part of the course.

Before you post, say what you tried and what you saw: the page you were on, the command or click, and the exact text of any error. A question with those three things gets a useful answer on the first reply.

Questions about your own grade, your own submission, or your own circumstances go to the Canvas Inbox, not here. So does any question about a specific quiz item.

This discussion is open all term. It is not graded.
