Forum Rubric

Grades the module discussion forum: a short case-study post plus a reply to a peer. Forums run on odd Modules 03 through 15, and each one covers one real-world incident whose root cause is its own module's topic. The forum is meant to be light. The depth belongs in the incident report; here the goal is to meet the failure, say what went wrong and how it could have been stopped, and engage a classmate. The Module 01 introductions forum is graded complete or incomplete, not with this rubric.

Five criteria at 20 points each, 100 total. Levels: None 0 · Partial 1 = 10 · Partial 2 = 15 · Full = 20.

Criterion 3 rewards precision about this incident over general statements about security. Two accurate sentences that name the real mechanism earn full marks.

How to use this rubric

Read the criteria before starting, then check your submission against the full-credit descriptions. Only the sections and tests required by the assignment are assessed. Supplied case facts are background, not evidence that you performed a test. Explain required unknowns rather than inventing results.
